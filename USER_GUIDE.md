# FactoryEase — Simple Guide (1 page)

**App name:** FactoryEase  
**For:** Factory owner (daily use on phone)

---

## First time

1. Open **FactoryEase**.
2. Sign in with the email and password your son set up.
3. Optional: at the bottom, choose **हिंदी** or **English**.

If the screen says you are **offline**, you can still add expenses and mark attendance. They will sync when the internet returns (yellow banner).

---

## Every day (owner)

### 1) See today
Open the **Today** tab. Check:
- How many people are **Present**
- **Expenses today**
- **This month** expenses

### 2) Mark attendance
Open **Attendance** → **Today**.  
For each worker, tap **Present**, **Absent**, or **Half day** (big buttons).

### 3) Add an expense
**Today** → **Add Expense**, or **Expenses** → **Add expense**.
1. Type the **amount**
2. Tap a **category** (Raw Material, Labor, Transport, Utility, Other)
3. Optional short note
4. Optional **Take photo** of the bill
5. Tap **Save**

### 4) Make an invoice
**Documents** → **Invoices** → **New invoice**  
Pick customer → fill items → choose GST % → **Save & preview** → **Share PDF** (WhatsApp / Files).

### 5) Make a delivery challan
**Documents** → **Challans** → **New challan**  
Same idea, simpler (no rates). Share PDF when ready.

### 6) Monthly report
**Reports** tab → check totals → **Share / export PDF** if needed.

---

## Employees (workers with login)

They only see **My attendance**:
- **Check In** when work starts  
- **Check Out** when work ends  

---

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|--------|------------|
| Cannot sign in | Check email/password. Ask for a password reset. |
| Yellow “offline” banner | Keep working. Wait for network; changes sync alone. |
| No employees in attendance | Ask admin to add employees and link logins. |
| Photo will not open | Allow Camera / Photos permission in phone Settings. |
| Share PDF missing WhatsApp | Use **Share** → pick WhatsApp, Files, or Drive. |

---

## Do not

- Do not uninstall the app if offline items are waiting to sync.  
- Do not share your login password.  
- Do not delete an expense unless you are sure.
