# PolyOps privacy notice

## Factory records

PolyOps stores factory memberships, user email addresses, material catalogs, available stock, buyer company names and contact numbers, order history and inventory movements. Better Auth manages sign-in and sessions using the application's Postgres database.

## Access and processing

Factory admins manage their factory's commercial records. Staff can read material quantities and buyer names, receive goods, pack orders and record dispatch. Staff order responses omit prices and buyer phone numbers. Access is scoped to the authenticated factory.

The optional weekly brief sends aggregate figures and material names to Gemini. Keep personal information out of material names. Request logging excludes credentials, contact numbers and request bodies.

## Pilot contact and retention

The operator must publish a privacy contact, retention period, hosting arrangements and backup expiry before onboarding a factory. Factory administrators should direct access or removal requests to that contact. The operator verifies the request and handles business records, sign-in records and retained backups according to the published policy.
